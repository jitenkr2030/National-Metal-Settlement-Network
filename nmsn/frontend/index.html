How It Works

Simple 4-step process for metal payments

1

User Chooses Metal Payment

Customer selects Gold, Silver, or Platinum payment option at checkout.

2

Real-time Conversion

System calculates exact metal amount needed based on live LBMA/MCX prices.

3

Token Burn & BINR Mint

Metal tokens are burned, equivalent BINR stablecoin is minted for settlement.

4

Instant INR Settlement

BINR automatically converts to INR and settles to merchant's bank account.
