Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about NMSN.

How quickly can we start accepting metal payments? +
Most merchants can go live within 24-48 hours after completing KYC verification and basic integration. We provide a sandbox environment for testing and dedicated support during onboarding.
What are the settlement times for metal payments? +
All metal payments settle to INR within 2 seconds. The settlement process automatically converts metal tokens to BINR stablecoin and then to INR in the merchant's bank account.
What metals can customers pay with? +
Customers can pay with Gold (BGT), Silver (BST), Platinum (BPT), and Metal Basket Tokens (MBT). We support all major precious metals with real-time pricing from LBMA, MCX, and COMEX exchanges.
What are the fees for merchants? +
Network fees range from 0.2% to 0.5% per transaction, depending on volume and plan. There are no setup fees for basic integration, and we provide transparent pricing with no hidden charges.
Is NMSN compliant with Indian regulations? +
Yes, NMSN is fully compliant with RBI, SEBI, and FIU guidelines. We handle all regulatory reporting, AML/KYC verification, and suspicious activity monitoring automatically as part of our platform.
Do you provide mobile apps or SDKs? +
We provide React Native mobile apps for iOS and Android, REST APIs, JavaScript SDKs, and plugins for popular e-commerce platforms like Shopify and WooCommerce.
