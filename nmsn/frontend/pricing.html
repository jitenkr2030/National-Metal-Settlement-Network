Pricing FAQ

Common questions about our pricing structure.

Are there any hidden fees? +
No hidden fees whatsoever. The price you see is what you pay. We charge a transparent network fee per transaction plus the license fee for your chosen plan.
What happens if I exceed my transaction limit? +
If you exceed your monthly limit, you'll be charged the standard network fee of 0.5% for additional transactions. You can upgrade your plan anytime for better rates.
Can I change plans later? +
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any billing differences.
Is there a setup fee for Enterprise plans? +
Enterprise plans include white-glove onboarding at no additional cost. This includes dedicated integration support, custom feature development, and training.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept bank transfers, UPI, and major credit cards for license fees. For transaction settlements, you receive INR directly in your bank account.
Do you offer refunds? +
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for Professional and Enterprise plans. If you're not satisfied, we'll refund your license fee in full.
